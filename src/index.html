Scenes

A scene saves your whole Voicemeeter setup — faders, routing, devices — so you can switch setups in one click.

        Level history

        Nothing recorded yet. Leave Sleekmeeter running and the graph fills in as sound plays.

        Each line is the loudest reading of that channel in every few seconds. The red line is 0 dB: above it a channel distorts. History is only kept while Sleekmeeter runs.

        App volumes

        Turn one app up or down without touching the rest, or mute it. These are the same controls as Windows' volume mixer, so the app is lowered before Voicemeeter hears it. The box on the right chooses which output the app plays to, for example one of Voicemeeter's virtual inputs.

        Stream mode: Be right back

        The BRB button (or a shortcut, or an OBS scene) mutes your mic, turns the game down and plays music, all with fades. Press it again and everything goes back exactly as it was.

        Pick channels above, then try the BRB button. Add a shortcut for it under Shortcuts.

        OBS Studio

        In OBS: Tools → WebSocket Server Settings → Enable WebSocket server. Sleekmeeter only listens; it never changes anything in OBS. Twitch streams are covered: OBS tells Sleekmeeter when you go live.

        Twitch chat commands

        Phone remote

        Open the address below on your phone. The long code in it is the password: anyone on your network who has the address can use the remote, so don't share it. Nothing goes over the internet. Windows may ask once to allow Sleekmeeter on your network; choose private networks.

        A new key signs every phone out until you open the new address on it.

        Backup and layout

        Save your settings to a file to move them to another PC or keep a safe copy, and restore them later. Your scenes are the .xml files in the scenes folder next to Sleekmeeter; copy that folder too. The OBS password is never saved to the file.
