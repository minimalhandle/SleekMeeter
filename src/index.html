                          Help count Sleekmeeter users?

                          Once a day, Sleekmeeter would send sleekmeeter.app a random ID, its version number and your Voicemeeter edition. Nothing else: no names, devices, settings or audio. It only tells us how many people use Sleekmeeter, so we know where to spend time. You can change this any time in Settings → Privacy.

                          Stream mode: Be right back

                          The BRB button (or a shortcut, or an OBS scene) mutes your mic, turns the game down and plays music, all with fades. Press it again and everything goes back exactly as it was.

                          Pick channels above, then try the BRB button. Add a shortcut for it under Shortcuts.

                          Twitch chat commands

                          Sleekmeeter joins your chat as a silent viewer: no sign-in, and it never posts. Only the commands you add below do anything, and only for the people you allow.

                          Chat saysDoesOn

                          Leave the last box empty to use what's typed after the command, like !scene followed by a scene name or !sound followed by a sound name.

                          Channel point rewards

                          Play a sound (or anything above) when a viewer redeems a reward. On Twitch, tick Require viewer to enter text for the reward, since only those show up in chat. Anyone who redeems it can run it, with the same wait as commands.

                          Phone remote

                          Open the address below on your phone. The long code in it is the password: anyone on your network who has the address can use the remote, so don't share it. Nothing goes over the internet. Windows may ask once to allow Sleekmeeter on your network; choose private networks.

                          For OBS: add a Browser source and paste this address. It shows a MUTED or BRB badge, your mic meter and the name of the sound that just played, on a see-through background. Add &mic=1 if your mic is not the first channel.

                          A new key signs every phone out until you open the new address on it.

                          Mic clean-up and plugins
                          Switched off by a shortcut, the Stream Deck, the phone, chat or a scene.

                          Stereo mode keeps left and right separate for capture cards and other hardware sources. Use VST3 effects with stereo input and output. NVIDIA mic effects are available in mono mode.

                          NVIDIA's clean-up needs an NVIDIA RTX graphics card and NVIDIA's free Audio Effects SDK runtime, which isn't installed. Get it from NVIDIA's Maxine Audio Effects page, then come back here.

                          VST3 plugins

                          Add effects such as an EQ or compressor. They run in the order shown, after any NVIDIA mic effects.

                          Portable chain preset

                          Move this mic’s NVIDIA effects, VST3 order and plugin settings to another PC. Import shows a compatibility preview before replacing the selected chain.

                          All of this happens inside Voicemeeter before its fader, gate and compressor, so it reaches every output, and adds a little delay (shown above). It runs while Sleekmeeter is open; if Sleekmeeter closes, your mic carries on without it. NVIDIA's clean-up needs Voicemeeter at 48 kHz. Plugins run in a separate helper, so one that crashes doesn't take Sleekmeeter with it. Switch a mic's clean-up off and on with a shortcut, a Stream Deck key, the phone remote or a chat command, and scenes remember it.

                          Backup and layout

                          Save your settings to a file to move them to another PC or keep a safe copy, and restore them later. Your scenes are the .xml files in the scenes folder next to Sleekmeeter; copy that folder too. The OBS password is never saved to the file.

                          Voicemeeter backups

                          Sleekmeeter saves a copy of Voicemeeter's whole setup once a day, and before loading a scene, running the setup assistant or restoring a backup. Restoring puts that setup back; your current one is saved first.
